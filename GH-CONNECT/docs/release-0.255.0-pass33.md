# GreenHaven 0.255.0 — Pass 33

## Contextual journey guidance

Pass 33 adds compact, category-aware journey hints to discovery cards for communities, events, activities and services. Each hint explains a sensible sequence of review and participation steps, with conditional wording where availability or provider messaging may vary. This reduces uncertainty at the point of discovery and helps users understand what to expect before opening a destination.

The hints are guidance only. They do not track or claim completed progress, promise that an action is available, create orders, authorize payments, change balances, or replace server responses. Discovery card actions and all backend authority remain unchanged.

Canonical navigation remains Home · Messages · Communities · Cart · Profile. Wallet remains an overlay. No Supabase migrations are changed.
