# GreenHaven Research Studio — owner quick guide

## Create a research project

1. Sign in to GreenHaven.
2. Open **Ecosystem → Research & Intelligence → Field Research**. This opens Research Studio at `/field/studio`.
3. Select **Create research**.
4. Choose Survey, Examination, Election Observation, Registration or Custom Form.
5. Give the project a clear title and purpose.
6. Add, edit and remove questions with the visual builder. Choose short answer, paragraph, single choice, checkboxes, dropdown, rating, number or date. Mark required questions and enter choice options on separate lines.
7. Select **Preview** to test the layout and answer controls.
8. Save the draft, then choose **Publish form** when it is ready.
9. Copy the unique public URL or use Share, WhatsApp, Facebook or GreenHaven Chat. GreenHaven Chat opens Messages and copies the URL so the owner can paste it into a conversation.
10. Open **Responses** on the project card to review submissions in the private Research Inbox.

## Public respondents

Anyone who has the published URL can open it in a modern browser. They do not need a GreenHaven account, Pi Browser or Field Research agent access code. They must complete required questions and confirm the consent statement before submission.

## Field agents

The separate `/field` portal remains for access-code-authorized field agents. It does not grant GreenHaven account, wallet or administrator privileges.

## Important boundaries in this release

- Election Observation is a field-reporting and data-collection workflow. Public submissions are not certified or official election results and do not replace an election authority's process.
- Examination currently provides a form template and response capture. Timed sessions, randomized questions, automatic marking and grade release are not yet enabled.
- Image, video, audio and document evidence uploads are not yet enabled in the Research Studio response flow.
- Conditional branching, saved draft editing/version history, geographic master data, candidate roster management, automatic tally reconciliation, response exports and admin chat notifications remain planned upgrades.
- Do not collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary, lawful and clearly disclosed to respondents.
