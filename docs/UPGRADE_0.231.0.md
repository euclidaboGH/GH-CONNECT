# GreenHaven 0.231.0 — Massive Upgrade Pass 9

## Opportunities & Matching Results

This pass turns Matches from a passive list into an action-oriented opportunity surface while preserving the core safety rule: mutual interest is not a connection, friendship, compatibility claim, or automatic chat relationship.

### Product upgrades
- Added a Match Action Centre with mutual-match and inbound-interest visibility.
- Added direct pathways to Find people and review connection requests.
- Added a practical best-next-step explanation to reduce ambiguity and impulsive connection behavior.
- Preserved intention filtering for professional, collaboration, mentorship, learning, friendship, dating and shared-interest contexts.
- Renamed the Rewards Centre `Earn` tab to `Opportunities` to better reflect legitimate participation pathways without implying guaranteed income.
- Reworded opportunity counts as participation paths rather than earnings claims.

### Authority preserved
Matching, connection requests, blocks, user identity, rewards, financial balances and membership remain governed by their existing authoritative systems. This pass does not invent compatibility scores, reward amounts, income, verification or financial outcomes.
